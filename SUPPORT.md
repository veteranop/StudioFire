# Support

StudioFire is free, self-hosted radio automation. Here is exactly what support
exists, so nobody has to guess.

## Your own station first

Every install has a **support contact** — the person or company who set
StudioFire up for you. It is shown on the **Help** page and in error banners.

* Set or change it in **Settings → Station → Support contact**.
* If it is blank, Help says: *“Ask whoever set up StudioFire for your station.”*

Most problems are configuration, and whoever installed it can fix them fastest.

## Community support (free)

| Where | For |
|---|---|
| [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/veteranop/StudioFire/issues) | Bug reports, reproducible faults, feature requests |
| [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/veteranop/StudioFire/discussions) | Questions, “how do I…”, sharing setups |

When reporting a bug, please include: StudioFire version (Settings → About &
updates), Windows version, what you expected, what happened, and the relevant
lines from `logs\` (the Setup page names the folder). Never post stream
passwords or your music library paths if they identify your station.

**There is no guaranteed response time on community support.** It is best-effort,
by maintainers and other users.

## Paid support (optional)

Installation, configuration, training, and managed operation for your station are
available from [VeteranOp, LLC](https://veteranop.com). That is a separate
commercial agreement; nothing in this repository obliges anyone to provide it.

Maintainers: put your support address, hours and SLA (if any) here before
publishing a paid offering.

## What support does not cover

* Your music licensing and reporting obligations — StudioFire produces the
  paperwork, but it is a tool, not legal advice. Confirm your licence type and
  reporting option with SoundExchange (or your licensing body).
* Your transmitter, audio chain hardware, or network — the equipment page can
  tell you what is not answering, not why.
* Recovering a music library that was deleted outside StudioFire.
