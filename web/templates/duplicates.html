Duplicate cleanup

Finds songs that appear more than once in the music library (same file name, ignoring a "(1)" or "- copy" on the end). Look first, then choose. Anything you leave unticked is moved — not deleted — into a _Duplicates folder inside your music library, so it can always be brought back. Songs used by a playlist, a show or a spot are protected and won't be moved.
